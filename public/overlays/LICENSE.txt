Source: OpenStreetMap via Overpass API. The exact query, source timestamp,
endpoint, and response SHA-256 are retained in switzerland.json.

Selection: named settlement points within the OSM Swiss country boundary tagged
place=city, place=town, or place=village. Local names are preserved. Population
is included only when an integer can be parsed without guessing. No elevations,
terrain queries, geocoding queries, or rendered labels are included in this file.

When displaying these data on a map, include visible OpenStreetMap attribution
linked to https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.
